💡 How to follow: A five-movement athletic-strength reference: unilateral force, bilateral force, eccentric hamstrings, deceleration, and a loaded squat. The reel supplies intent but no full dosage, so follow the demonstrations and program the load separately.
1

Split-Stance Overcoming Isometric Pull

Unilateral force
Not specified in reel

Drive the front leg into an immovable bar or pin from a split stance. The overlay frames this as zero-to-max force for takeoff-angle strength.

  1. Set a stable split stance.
  2. Push through the front leg.
  3. Build force progressively rather than jerking.
  4. Stop if the setup moves or position collapses.
2

Trap-Bar Overcoming Isometric Pull

Bilateral force
Not specified in reel

Push through the floor against an immovable trap bar with both legs. The reel emphasizes maximal bilateral force and a two-foot takeoff pattern.

  1. Brace before pulling.
  2. Push the floor away.
  3. Keep both feet rooted.
  4. Use a conservative effort until the setup is coached.
3

Nordic Curl

Hamstrings
Not specified in reel

Lower forward from the knees and fight the fall under control. The reel presents the eccentric as a way to build hamstring resilience.

  1. Anchor the feet securely.
  2. Keep the hips extended.
  3. Fight the descent rather than dropping.
  4. Use assistance if control is lost.
4

Trap-Bar Deadlift Resisted Isometric

Deceleration
Not specified in reel

Use a resisted pull or isometric position to practice absorbing force and freezing the landing position. Exact load and setup are not provided.

  1. Use a stable resisted setup.
  2. Decelerate under control.
  3. Freeze the catch position.
  4. Do not turn the demo into an uncoached maximal lift.
5

Barbell Squat

Strength base
Not specified in reel

The reel closes with a loaded squat demonstration. Stance, depth, sets, and reps are intentionally left unspecified.

  1. Brace before descending.
  2. Keep the bar path controlled.
  3. Use a depth you can own.
  4. Rack the bar safely if position degrades.
