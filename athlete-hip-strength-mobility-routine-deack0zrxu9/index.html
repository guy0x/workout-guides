💡 How to follow: Six bodyweight hip-strength and mobility drills shown in sequence. The reel does not specify a dose, so use 1–2 controlled rounds of 5–8 reps per side and stay in a pain-free range.
1

Seated Hip Flexor Lift

Hip flexor strength
Not specified in reel · suggested: 5–8 reps/side

Active straight-leg lift from the seated position shown. Treat it as a controlled hip-flexor drill, not a momentum kick.

  1. Sit tall with the working leg long and the pelvis level.
  2. Lift from the hip without leaning the torso backward.
  3. Keep the knee straight only as far as you can control.
  4. Lower slowly and keep the range pain-free.
2

Side Hip Flexor Opener

Hip opening
Not specified in reel · suggested: 5–8 controlled reps/side

The seated side-position hip opener labelled in the reel. Keep the trunk organized while exploring the available hip range.

  1. Set up in the seated position shown before moving.
  2. Keep the supporting side grounded and the movement deliberate.
  3. Do not force the knee or twist through a pinching sensation.
  4. Return to the start with control and repeat on the other side.
3

90/90 Leg Swing

Hip rotation mobility
Not specified in reel · suggested: 5–8 reps/side

Controlled leg movement from the 90/90 setup. Use the smallest range that keeps the torso and pelvis steady.

  1. Start in the 90/90 position shown in the reel.
  2. Stay tall rather than collapsing into the hips.
  3. Swing or sweep the leg smoothly; do not bounce into end range.
  4. Keep both sides of the pelvis as quiet as possible.
4

90/90 Sit Rotation Up

Hip rotation + control
Not specified in reel · suggested: 5–8 reps/side

A 90/90 rotational transition with an active rise, labelled ‘90/90 Sit Rotation Up’ in the reel.

  1. Begin in the demonstrated 90/90 position.
  2. Rotate from the hips while keeping the motion smooth.
  3. Rise only as high as you can without pushing off or wrenching the knees.
  4. Reverse the path slowly and switch sides.
5

Table Top Leg Kick

Hip extension + trunk control
Not specified in reel · suggested: 5–8 reps/side

From the tabletop position shown, extend one leg while resisting rotation through the pelvis and ribcage.

  1. Set hands and knees under the shoulders and hips.
  2. Brace lightly so the low back does not sway as the leg moves.
  3. Kick or extend through the hip, not by throwing the leg.
  4. Return the knee under control and repeat on the other side.
6

Hip Lateral Abduction

Lateral hip strength
Not specified in reel · suggested: 5–8 reps/side

Side-lying leg lift labelled ‘Hip Lateral Abduction.’ Keep the pelvis stacked while the upper leg moves away from midline.

  1. Lie on your side with the hips stacked and the lower knee comfortable.
  2. Lift the top leg without rolling the pelvis backward.
  3. Keep the toes and knee aligned with the direction shown.
  4. Lower slowly and complete both sides.
