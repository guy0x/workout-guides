Standing Hip Kick — Own the Active Range

Drill 1
Reference flow; no source dosage

Stand tall and raise/extend one leg in a controlled side-kick pattern. Keep the range active and return without swinging or collapsing into the stance hip.

  1. Brace lightly through the trunk
  2. Keep the standing foot grounded
  3. Move only through a pain-free range
  4. Control the return
2

Side Plank Leg Extension — Stabilize the Hip

Drill 2
Reference flow; no source dosage

Use a side-facing or angled forearm-plank position and extend or lift the top leg while keeping the trunk organized.

  1. Keep the shoulder stacked over the forearm
  2. Avoid rolling the pelvis backward
  3. Use a knee-down regression if needed
  4. Stop before the low back twists
3

Supported High-Knee Balance — Control Hip Rotation

Drill 3
Reference flow; no source dosage

Hold a stable post or wall for balance, chamber one knee high, and control the hip as the leg moves through its available range.

  1. Use a truly stable support
  2. Keep the pelvis level
  3. Do not yank the knee upward
  4. Own both the lift and the return
4

Frog/Straddle Stretch — Open the Hips Gently

Drill 4
Reference flow; no source dosage

Move into a wide-knee frog or straddle-based hip-opening position with the hands supporting the torso. Stay in a calm, pain-free range.

  1. Use a mat or non-slip surface
  2. Keep the breath steady
  3. Do not bounce or force the groin
  4. Exit slowly
