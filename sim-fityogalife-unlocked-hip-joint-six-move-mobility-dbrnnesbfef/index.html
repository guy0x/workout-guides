💡 How to follow: A six-exercise hip-mobility routine with visible repetition counts: butterfly opening, straddle side reaches, bilateral seated reaches, a butterfly forward-fold variation, seated knee-tuck/hip-flexion control, and bilateral seated leg-extension mobility. The source provides reps but no tempo, rest, or rounds.
1

Butterfly Hip Opener — Move the Knees with Control

Exercise 1
15 reps

Use the seated/reclined butterfly-style opening shown in the reel. Move the knees or hips through a controlled range rather than bouncing into the groin.

  1. Keep the spine supported
  2. Move smoothly
  3. Do not force the knees down
  4. Keep both feet stable
2

Straddle Side Reach — Open the Inner Thighs

Exercise 2
15 reps

Sit in a wide-leg straddle and reach side-to-side as demonstrated. Keep the movement active and lengthen through the torso instead of collapsing.

  1. Sit as tall as range allows
  2. Reach from the ribs
  3. Keep the knees facing up
  4. Avoid forcing the split
3

Bilateral Seated Reach — Rotate Through Each Hip

Exercise 3
15 reps with both legs

With one leg extended and the other bent, reach toward the extended side as shown, then repeat on both legs.

  1. Repeat evenly on both sides
  2. Keep the extended leg active
  3. Rotate without wrenching the knee
  4. Breathe through the reach
4

Butterfly Forward Fold — Hinge from the Hips

Exercise 4
15 reps

From a butterfly position, fold forward through the hips and return with control. Use a smaller range if the back rounds aggressively.

  1. Lead with the chest
  2. Keep the movement gentle
  3. Do not pull on the knees
  4. Return slowly
5

Seated Knee Tuck — Control Hip Flexion

Exercise 5
20 reps

Balance or support the torso while drawing the knees toward the chest, then return with control. The source shows a seated knee-tuck position rather than a sustained hold.

  1. Keep the trunk organized
  2. Use hands for support if needed
  3. Avoid momentum
  4. Shorten the range if the back strains
6

Single-Leg Seated Stretch — Repeat Both Legs

Exercise 6
15 times with both legs

Extend one leg and bend the other as shown, holding or reaching toward the bent-leg foot/shin. Repeat the movement with both legs.

  1. Complete both sides
  2. Keep the extended leg relaxed but long
  3. Do not yank the foot toward you
  4. Stay within a comfortable stretch
