💡 How to follow: One morning flow from 5 coaches: dead hang, deep squat, couch stretch, 90/90 switches, APT reset (reverse lean · half-camel · tabletop lift · psoas rock · hamstring fold), golf hip CARs (standing · quadruped · seated), big-toe drive, kettlebell halos. ~12 min.
1

Dead Hang + Leg Swings

Spine
30 sec

Hang from a bar, swing your legs — decompresses the spine and releases the lower back in real time.

  1. Grip the bar, full hang
  2. Swing legs forward/back gently
  3. Keep shoulders engaged, no dead weight
  4. 30 seconds
2

Deep Squat Hold

Hips / Ankles
2 min

A two-minute daily squat undoes hours of sitting damage and opens the hips.

  1. Sit all the way down, heels flat
  2. Elbows inside knees, pry them open
  3. Chest tall, breathe
  4. 2 minutes total
3

Couch Stretch

Hip flexors / Quads
60 sec / side

Back shin vertical on a bench, front leg in lunge stance — releases the tight hip flexors pulling on your lower back.

  1. Back shin up on bench, foot relaxed
  2. Front foot forward into 90° lunge
  3. Push hips forward, upright torso
  4. 60 sec per side
4

90/90 Hip Switches

Hips / Rotation
10 slow reps

Sit with knees bent, plant hands behind — sweep both knees side to side to unlock hip rotation.

  1. Hands behind, knees together
  2. Sweep knees to one side (90/90)
  3. Smoothly switch to the other side
  4. 10 slow, controlled reps
5

Kneeling Reverse Lean

Hip flexors
8–10 reps

Tall kneeling, lean the whole body back as one block — stretches tight hip flexors that tip the pelvis forward.

  1. Tall kneeling, torso rigid
  2. Lean back toward heels, hinge only at knees
  3. Keep spine neutral
  4. 8–10 slow reps
6

Half-Camel Reach

Front functional line
5 breaths / side

One hand back to the heel, opposite arm sweeps up and across — opens the entire diagonal front chain.

  1. Upright kneeling
  2. Reach one hand back to your heel/ankle
  3. Sweep the other arm overhead and across
  4. 5 deep breaths per side
7

Reverse Tabletop Leg Lift

Glutes / Hamstrings
10 / side

Seated tabletop lean-back with leg extensions — wakes up the glutes and hamstrings that counter-hold the pelvis.

  1. Lean back into reverse tabletop
  2. Keep hips high
  3. Extend one leg, lower with control
  4. 10 per side
8

Psoas Lean → Toe Squat Rock

Psoas
10 reps

Kneel with toes tucked, lean back onto hands, then rock forward into a deep toe squat — psoas mobility in motion.

  1. Kneel, toes tucked under
  2. Lean torso back, hands behind
  3. Rock forward into a deep toe squat
  4. Flow back and forth, 10 reps
9

Half-Kneeling Hamstring Fold

Hamstrings
30–45 sec / side

Half-split position, hinge forward over the front leg — hamstrings balance the pelvis pull from the front.

  1. One knee down, front leg extended
  2. Toes up, heel on floor
  3. Hinge forward at the hips, long spine
  4. 30–45 sec per side
10

Standing Hip CARs (Open / Close the Gate)

Hips / Rotation
5–8 slow / side

Single-leg stance beside a hip-height hurdle: sweep the working leg over the hurdle forward (open) and backward (close) through full rotation.

  1. Stand side-on to a foam roller/hurdle
  2. Lift knee to 90°, sweep it over the roller
  3. Sweep back the same way
  4. 5–8 each direction per side
11

Quadruped Hip CARs

Hips / Control
5–8 slow / side

Tabletop position, tracing controlled hip circles — rotation quality without the balance requirement.

  1. All fours, knees under hips
  2. Lift one knee, keep hips level
  3. Trace circles through full hip range
  4. 5–8 each direction per side
12

Seated Hip CARs

Hips / End-range
5–8 slow / side

Seated, rotating the hip open and closed in controlled arcs — end-range hip rotation for golfers and sitters.

  1. Sit tall, one leg out
  2. Rotate the hip open, then close
  3. Spine stays tall, no leaning
  4. 5–8 arcs per side
13

Big-Toe Extension + Knee Drives

Feet / Ankles
10 / side

Barefoot, big toe pressed up against a box edge, knee drives forward — stretch the plantar fascia and 1st MTP joint.

  1. Barefoot, big toe up against box/wall
  2. Ball of foot and heel stay flat
  3. Drive the knee forward over the toe
  4. Oscillate, 10 per side
14

Kettlebell Halos (The Angel Workout)

Full body / Rotation
10 each direction

Inverted kettlebell gripped by the horns, circled around the head — core, shoulders and spinal rotation in one move.

  1. Invert the KB, grip the horns
  2. Trace a halo circle around your head
  3. Core braced, spine tall
  4. 10 each direction
