💡 How to follow: A progressive knee-strengthening series: strengthen the quads in the sissy position, balance the front of the lower leg with tib work, and finish with a supported step-up. Go slow, control every rep.
1

Hackenschmidt Sissy Squat

Quad Strength

The foundational quad-focused sissy squat — knees track forward while the hips stay back, loading the quadriceps in deep flexion. The gateway exercise of the Hackenschmidt method.

  1. Lean back, knees travel forward
  2. Heels stay down, hips stay stacked
  3. Rise by extending the knees
2

Tibialis Anterior Wall Raise

Lower Leg Balance

Back-to-the-wall tibialis raise — lifting the front of the foot against resistance to strengthen the anterior shin, balancing the calf and protecting the knee from the outside in.

  1. Back flat against the wall
  2. Dorsiflex toes up and in
  3. Control the negative, slow reps
3

Slant-Board Poliquin Step-Up

Knee Stability

A Poliquin-style step-up with the foot on a slant board: drives the knee forward and over the toes to strengthen the vastus medialis and reinforce patellar tracking.

  1. Foot planted on the slant, knee tracks over toe
  2. Drive through the whole foot
  3. Lower with control, keep the pelvis level
