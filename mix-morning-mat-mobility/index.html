💡 Why this mix: A grounding 3-move morning reset on a mat (no equipment): deep squat hold to open hips and ankles, 90/90 switches for pelvic rotation, and the chair-assisted posterior-tilt breath to re-align posture before the day. Posture, pelvis, and gait all get a wake-up in 10 minutes.
1

Deep Squat Hold

Hips / Ankles
needs gym
2 min

A two-minute daily squat undoes hours of sitting damage and opens the hips.

  1. Sit all the way down, heels flat
  2. Elbows inside knees, pry them open
  3. Chest tall, breathe
  4. 2 minutes total

2

90/90 Hip Switches

Hips / Rotation
needs gym
10 slow reps

Sit with knees bent, plant hands behind — sweep both knees side to side to unlock hip rotation.

  1. Hands behind, knees together
  2. Sweep knees to one side (90/90)
  3. Smoothly switch to the other side
  4. 10 slow, controlled reps

3

Drag Heels → Posterior Tilt

Pelvis
office-safe
Gentle, ~2/10

Drag down into the chair with the back of your heels. Hamstrings engage, the pelvis moves into a posterior tilt, and the low back flattens. Very gentle — about 2 out of 10 effort.

  1. Drag heels down into the chair
  2. Hamstrings engage
  3. Pelvis rolls into posterior tilt
  4. Low back flattens — 2/10 effort

4

Breath — 2×10 Slow

Breathe
office-safe
2 sets × 10 breaths

Exhale gently through an open mouth with a relaxed jaw for 5–10 seconds until the side abs engage. Pause, hold them gently, then inhale through the nose, letting the air fall in. Slow and passive — the front of the rib cage opens more each breath, no neck engagement.

  1. Exhale open-mouth 5–10s, jaw relaxed
  2. Feel the side abs engage at the end
  3. Pause, then inhale through nose
  4. Rib cage opens in front — no neck tension
  5. Repeat: 2 sets × 10 slow breaths
