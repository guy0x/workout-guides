💡 How to follow: A kettlebell conditioning circuit for wrestler-style power and work capacity. Four exercises, 30 seconds × 2 sets each: rotate, press, row, shrug. Move through full range and keep the bell controlled.
1

Kettlebell Arm Rotation

Shoulder Mobility

Controlled arm rotation with the kettlebell around the shoulder — building rotation, mobility, and the shoulder-to-bell connection wrestlers need for control under load.

  1. Slow arc around the shoulder
  2. Keep the wrist tight, bell controlled
  3. 30s × 2 sets, keep the arc smooth
2

Kettlebell Shoulder Press

Press

Strict overhead press from the shoulders — building pressing power and lockout strength straight from the harness position.

  1. Hold at shoulder height first
  2. Press strict to full lockout
  3. 30s × 2 sets, controlled negative
3

Kettlebell Gorilla Row

Row

Gorilla-style row — a low, explosive pull to the midline on each side, building lat power and total-body stability.

  1. Hinge low, knee-driven stance
  2. Row the bell hard to the lats
  3. 30s × 2 sets, stay braced
4

Kettlebell Shrug

Traps

Heavy shrug off the shelf — short, sharp trap drive that finishes the circuit and builds the shrug strength wrestlers rely on in the clinch.

  1. Bell by the sides, hips braced
  2. Shrug shoulders hard to ears
  3. 30s × 2 sets, squeeze and down
