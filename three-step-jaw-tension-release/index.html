Three-Step Jaw Tension Release

💡 How to follow: 3 movements · 30 seconds each · no equipment. Keep your jaw unclenched and pressure comfortable. Stop for pain, locking, numbness, dizziness, or worsening TMJ/dental symptoms.
1

Jawbone Thumb Release

Jaw tension
30 seconds per side

Use your thumb to apply comfortable pressure along the lower edge of the jawbone, slowly working toward the area beneath and behind the ear.

  1. Start with a relaxed jaw and unclenched teeth.
  2. Press along the jawbone with firm-but-comfortable pressure.
  3. Move slowly toward the back of the jaw; do not force a sore area.
2

Behind-Jaw Fingertip Massage

Jaw angle
30 seconds per side

Place three fingers behind the angle of the jaw just below the ear, open the mouth gently, then massage up and down.

  1. Find the soft area behind the jaw angle, below the ear.
  2. Open the mouth gently; no forced range or clenching.
  3. Massage up and down with light-to-moderate pressure.
3

Front-of-Ear Upward Massage

Pre-auricular area
30 seconds per side

Gently lift the tissue above the ear with the opposite hand, then massage immediately in front of the ear upward with the thumb.

  1. Use the opposite hand to gently lift above the ear.
  2. Place the working thumb just in front of the ear.
  3. Massage from bottom to top; keep every stroke comfortable.
