💡 How to follow: 💡 How to follow: Do this workout every other day until you reach your desired full range in the deep squat. Hold each position with control — never let your knees cave in (applies to every exercise).
1

Deep Squat Hold

Foundation Holds
Not specified in reel

The base position for the whole program. Feet about shoulder-width, turned slightly outward, back straight, weight over the middle of the foot, heels down. Hold for 60 seconds.

  1. Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out
  2. Back straight, chest up
  3. Weight mid-foot — don't lift heels
  4. Hold 60s
2

Deep Squat Shifts

Foundation Holds
Not specified in reel

From the deep squat, shift your weight side to side. Keep knees and feet in line — never let the knees cave in.

  1. Shift weight L↔R in the hole
  2. Knees follow feet throughout
  3. Control, no bouncing
3

Narrow Deep Squat Hold

Foundation Holds
Not specified in reel

Take a narrow stance (feet closer together) and hold a deep squat for 60 seconds.

  1. Narrow stance, feet close
  2. Hold deep squat 60s
  3. Heels stay planted
4

Staggered Deep Squat Hold

Foundation Holds
Not specified in reel

From the narrow stance, put one leg in front and hold the deep squat. Goal: fully extend one leg without assistance.

  1. One leg forward
  2. Extend the back leg
  3. Hold, relax into range
5

Single-Leg Knee Touches

Knee Touches
Not specified in reel

From a deep squat, touch the floor with your knee (dorsiflexion work) on one leg at a time.

  1. Deep squat position
  2. Knee touches floor
  3. One leg at a time
6

Knee Touches

Knee Touches
Not specified in reel

Turn your legs so feet point forward and touch both knees to the floor. Move with control rather than explosiveness.

  1. Feet pointing forward
  2. Both knees touch floor
  3. Controlled tempo
7

Deep Squat Overhead Reach

Spine Mobility
Not specified in reel

One hand on the floor, reach the other arm overhead. Opens the spine and upper back while staying in the squat.

  1. One hand to floor
  2. Reach opposite arm overhead
  3. Keep hips low
8

Deep Squat Forward Lean

Spine Mobility
Not specified in reel

Grab your feet with both hands and lean forward, aiming to touch your forehead to the floor. Use yoga blocks to track progress.

  1. Grab your feet
  2. Lean torso forward
  3. Aim forehead to floor
9

Internal Hip Rotation (Knee Touches)

Hip Rotation
Not specified in reel

Touch the floor with your knees without lifting either foot — internally rotates the hip.

  1. Knees to floor, feet planted
  2. Internal hip rotation
  3. Hips stay open
10

Horse Stance

Hip Rotation
Not specified in reel

Wider than shoulder-width stance, drop into a deep squat with straight back and engaged core, actively push hips outward.

  1. Wide stance
  2. Straight back, core engaged
  3. Push hips outward

Strength

Deep squat hold with weight — use the goblet position (or front squat with a barbell) and hold for 30 seconds.

One of the best whole-body mobility + strength moves: hold a deep squat with arms locked overhead.

Hero Pose Hold

Cooldown
Not specified in reel

Finish with hero pose (kneeling, sitting back between feet) and hold for 60 seconds to stretch the quads and ankles.
