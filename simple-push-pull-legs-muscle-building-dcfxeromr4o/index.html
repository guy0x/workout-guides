💡 How to follow: A three-day Push/Pull/Legs template repeated twice weekly. The creator specifies the exercise selection and split frequency, but not sets, reps, rest, or progression; the practical dose in this guide is clearly marked as ATHENA guidance.
1

Butterfly / Machine Chest Fly

Push · Chest
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 10–15

The reel opens its Push day with a butterfly machine chest fly.

  1. Set the seat so the handles line up with mid-chest.
  2. Keep a soft bend in the elbows and shoulders down.
  3. Bring the arms together without crashing the handles.
  4. Return under control and stop before the shoulder rolls forward.
2

Upper-Chest Press

Push · Upper chest
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 6–12

A chest press for the upper chest, described immediately after the butterfly movement.

  1. Set the handles around upper-chest height.
  2. Keep the shoulder blades supported and wrists stacked.
  3. Press without locking the elbows aggressively.
  4. Lower until the upper arms remain comfortable and controlled.
3

Shoulder Press

Push · Front delts
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 6–12

Overhead shoulder pressing for the anterior deltoids.

  1. Brace the trunk and keep the head neutral.
  2. Press in the machine’s natural path without shrugging.
  3. Use a range that keeps the elbows and shoulders comfortable.
  4. Lower slowly rather than dropping into the bottom position.
4

Lateral Raise

Push · Side delts
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 10–20

A side-delt raise, described as the next Push movement.

  1. Keep the ribs down and shoulders away from the ears.
  2. Lead with the elbows rather than swinging the hands.
  3. Raise only to a controlled shoulder-height range.
  4. Lower slowly and avoid bouncing out of the bottom.
5

Cable Triceps Extension

Push · Triceps
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 10–15

Cable triceps extensions finish the Push day.

  1. Keep the elbows close to the ribs or fixed in the demonstrated path.
  2. Extend without leaning your body over the cable.
  3. Squeeze the triceps at the bottom without snapping the elbows.
  4. Allow a controlled return to the starting position.
6

Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown or Pull-Up

Pull · Lats
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 6–12

The first Pull-day slot is a wide-grip lat pulldown or, as the creator offers, pull-ups.

  1. Choose the pulldown or pull-up version you can control.
  2. Start by drawing the shoulders down rather than yanking with the arms.
  3. Pull toward the upper chest or bring the chest toward the bar.
  4. Lower under control without losing ribcage and shoulder position.
7

T-Bar Row

Pull · Upper back
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 6–12

A T-bar row for the upper back, listed after the wide-grip vertical pull.

  1. Hinge with a stable spine and brace before pulling.
  2. Drive the elbows back without jerking the torso upright.
  3. Keep the chest supported or the hinge fixed, depending on the machine.
  4. Lower the load until the shoulder blades can move naturally.
8

One-Arm Row

Pull · Lat emphasis
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 8–12/side

A unilateral row used for a different lat emphasis, according to the creator.

  1. Support the body so the working shoulder can move freely.
  2. Pull the elbow toward the hip rather than straight up to the ribs.
  3. Keep the torso from rotating toward the weight.
  4. Reach under control before starting the next rep.
9

Biceps Curl

Pull · Biceps
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 8–15

Curls close the Pull day.

  1. Keep the upper arms quiet and the shoulders relaxed.
  2. Curl through the elbow without swinging the torso.
  3. Squeeze briefly near the top without losing wrist position.
  4. Lower slowly to a comfortable lengthened position.
10

Squat

Legs · Quads/glutes
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 6–12

Barbell squats begin the Legs day.

  1. Brace before descending and keep the bar path balanced over the mid-foot.
  2. Track the knees in line with the toes.
  3. Descend only as far as the current mobility and control allow.
  4. Stand by driving through the whole foot without folding the chest.
11

Seated Leg Curl

Legs · Hamstrings
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 8–15

A seated hamstring curl follows the squat.

  1. Align the machine pivot with the knee joint.
  2. Keep the hips pressed into the seat and brace the handles.
  3. Curl without lifting the pelvis or rushing the bottom.
  4. Return slowly until the hamstrings lengthen under control.
12

Leg Extension

Legs · Quadriceps
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 10–15

The creator then programs a leg extension for the quadriceps.

  1. Align the knee with the machine’s rotation axis.
  2. Keep the back supported and the hips down.
  3. Extend smoothly without kicking the pad.
  4. Lower under control and avoid forcing the lockout.
13

Romanian Deadlift

Legs · Glutes/hamstrings
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 6–12

The transcript’s unclear ‘ADLs’ is interpreted from context as RDLs: a hinge for the lower back, glutes, and posterior thigh.

  1. Push the hips back while keeping the spine long.
  2. Keep the weight close to the legs.
  3. Stop when the hamstrings are loaded without losing the hinge.
  4. Stand by driving the hips forward, not by leaning backward.
14

Standing Calf Raise

Legs · Calves
Not specified in reel · practical start: 2–4 × 10–20

Standing calf raises finish the Legs day.

  1. Stand tall and keep the weight balanced over the foot tripod.
  2. Rise through the big-toe side without rolling the ankles out.
  3. Pause briefly at the top if balance allows.
  4. Lower through a controlled range rather than bouncing.
