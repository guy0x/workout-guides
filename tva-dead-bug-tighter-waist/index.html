💡 How to follow: 95% of people never train the deepest layer of the abs — the TVA (transverse abdominis), the body's natural corset, whose job is pulling the stomach in while supporting the spine. Crunches don't target it. The dead bug does: it teaches the TVA to stabilize the spine while the limbs move. Cards 1–4 build the movement (setup → both sides → the all-limbs progression); card 5 is the full 10-minute circuit.
1

Setup — Flat Back, Knees 90°, Braced

Foundation

Lie flat on your back, knees at 90°, arms straight up over your face. Lower back flat on the ground — this is the whole exercise's reference position.

  1. Lie flat, knees at 90 degrees, arms straight over the face
  2. Press the lower back flat into the ground — no gap
  3. Engage the stomach as if somebody was about to drop a ball onto it
  4. Keep that brace for every rep that follows
2

Dead Bug — Right Arm + Left Leg

Main drill

The core drill itself: one arm and the opposite leg lower slowly toward the ground while the brace holds the spine still.

  1. Right arm and left leg move together
  2. Bring both slowly back towards the ground
  3. Exhale as you bring them back up
  4. Lower back stays flat the whole time — that's the TVA working
3

Dead Bug — Left Arm + Right Leg

Main drill

Same drill, other side. Down, back, pause, back up — the pause is where the deep core holds.

  1. Left arm and right leg move together
  2. Down, back, pause, back up
  3. Move slowly — speed hides the wobble that tells you the brace is lost
  4. Equal reps per side
4

Progression — All Four Limbs

Harder

The extra challenge: all four limbs lower and return at the same time. Only add this once both single sides are rock-steady.

  1. Bring all four limbs down at the same time
  2. Return them up together
  3. If the lower back arches off the floor, go back to one side at a time
  4. Exhale on the return
5

The Circuit — 5 Rounds, ~10 Minutes

Prescription

The full protocol: build to 5–10 per side (10–20 total reps per set), 60 seconds rest, five rounds through.

  1. 5–10 reps per side = 10–20 total reps per set
  2. 60 seconds rest between sets
  3. Repeat for 5 rounds
  4. ~10 minutes total — done consistently, this builds the deeper core for a tighter waist

Why this works

It targets the layer crunches miss. The TVA is the deepest abdominal layer — the natural corset that pulls the waist in and supports the spine. Surface ab work never loads it.

The lower back is the gauge. If the lower back leaves the floor during a rep, the TVA lost the brace. That's the signal to slow down or regress, not push through.

Slow beats many. Every limb movement is slow, with an exhale on the return. Speed just hands the work to the hip flexors.
