💡 How to follow: A run-focused hip progression: wake up the hips through a standing banded flexion, then drive the crossover pattern runners need for speed and change-of-direction. Control each rep.
1

Standing Banded Hip Flexion

Mobility & Drive

Standing hip flexion against a band builds the active hip lift runners need on every stride — training the lead knee to drive up with stiffness and control.

  1. Band anchored, pull against it
  2. Drive knee up to 90° with control
  3. Resist letting the band snap you back
  4. Repeat 10-12 per leg
2

Runner Crossover Hip Drive

Power

Crossover hip drive trains the rotational hip engine behind cutting and lateral acceleration — the step that lets a runner change direction sharply without losing the hips.

  1. Step across the midline, hip opens
  2. Drive the back hip forward aggressively
  3. Stay low, quick re-engage to the next step
