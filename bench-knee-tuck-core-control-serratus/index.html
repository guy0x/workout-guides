Bench Knee Tuck — Setup

Setup
3–4 sets across the week; source gives no rep target

Set up with the feet elevated on a flat bench and the hands planted on the floor. This creates the pike/plank base for a controlled knee-tuck pattern. Use a stable bench and enough clearance to keep the shoulders and wrists comfortable.

  1. Place the feet securely on the bench
  2. Plant the hands under the shoulders
  3. Brace before moving
  4. Use a stable surface
2

Bench Knee Tuck — Control the Rep

Drill 1
Controlled repetitions; stop before form breaks

From the elevated-foot position, draw the knees toward the chest and return with control. Keep tension where intended instead of rushing to reach a number, matching the creator’s primary cue.

  1. Pull the knees in under control
  2. Keep the trunk braced
  3. Move smoothly in both directions
  4. Do not rush the rep

Bench Knee Tuck — Core and Serratus Focus

Drill 2
Repeat only while the position stays controlled; no source rep count

The reel frames this movement as a core exercise with attention to the serratus anterior and visible abdominal definition. Treat the aesthetic language as motivational context, not a promise of spot reduction; progress only when the shoulders, spine, and pelvis remain controlled.

  1. Keep the ribs and pelvis organized
  2. Maintain steady shoulder support
  3. Keep tension in the trunk
  4. End the set when alignment fails
