💡 How to follow: 4 movements, no equipment, ~20 min. Foundation of the "Mastery in 20 Movements" series.
1

Hackenschmidt Sissy Squat

Quad · Knee tendon
3 × 8–10

The quad-and-knee-tendon builder — a deep, controlled negative on the front chain.

  1. Hold a wall/step for balance
  2. Knees travel forward as you lean back
  3. Descend slow, drive up through the quads
2

Tibialis Anterior Wall Raise

Shin · Knee stability
3 × 15

The forgotten shin muscle that stabilizes the knee and ankle — anti-injury insurance.

  1. Back flat against a wall, feet ~30cm out
  2. Flex toes toward the shins
  3. Rise onto the heels, control down
3

Slant-Board Poliquin Step-Up

VMO · Knee tracking
3 × 10 / side

The Poliquin step-up fires the VMO (inner quad) that tracks the kneecap — your office step works as the board.

  1. Heel of the front foot planted, toes straight
  2. Drive up through the front heel — don't push off the back foot
  3. Slow 3s lowering
4

Deep Squat Hold

Ankle · Hip mobility
3 × 30–45s

Own the bottom of the squat — the mobility base for every leg move.

  1. Feet ~shoulder-width, toes slightly out
  2. Sink to full depth, chest up
  3. Drive elbows between knees, stay tall
