Incline Dumbbell Curl

Arms

Dumbbell bicep curl on the incline — the arms fully hanging behind keeps the stretch deep and the curl growth stimulus high.

  1. Back on the incline, arms hang behind
  2. Supinate as you curl
  3. Squeeze, lower slow
2

Seated Dumbbell Overhead Press

Shoulders

Seated strict press to flat overhead — the upper-body killer move for the shoulder upward, clean and braced from the chair.

  1. Sit braced with back support
  2. Press to full lockout under control
  3. Lower to the start without missing
