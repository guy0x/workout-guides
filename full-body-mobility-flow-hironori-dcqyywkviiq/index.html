💡 How to follow: A one-minute music-only mobility flow that moves from a stool-supported posterior-chain fold through spinal waves, hip rocking, frog stretch, supine leg work, and a standing twist. No dosage is shown; use it as a gentle reset flow.
1

Stool-Supported Forward Fold / Elephant Walk

Posterior chain
Not specified in reel

Use two low stools or stable supports to regress the forward fold and explore the posterior chain without forcing the low back.

  1. Use stable supports.
  2. Hinge from the hips.
  3. Keep the neck relaxed.
  4. Stay within a comfortable hamstring range.
2

Quadruped Cat-Cow / Spinal Wave

Spine
Not specified in reel

From all fours, articulate the spine through a controlled wave. The source emphasizes segmental movement rather than speed.

  1. Place hands under shoulders.
  2. Move one spinal segment at a time.
  3. Coordinate breath naturally.
  4. Avoid forcing end range.
3

Quadruped Rocking Transition

Hips
Not specified in reel

Rock gently in quadruped as the flow transitions from spinal work toward the adductor opening.

  1. Keep the hands grounded.
  2. Move smoothly through the hips.
  3. Maintain a controlled trunk.
  4. Reduce the range if the back compensates.
4

Frog Stretch / Quadruped Adductor Opening

Adductors
Not specified in reel

Widen the knees and use the hands for support to explore the inner-thigh and groin range.

  1. Keep the spine comfortable.
  2. Support the torso with the hands.
  3. Do not force the groin range.
  4. Stop for sharp hip or groin pain.
5

Supine Leg Raises / Kicks

Core
Not specified in reel

Lie on the back and move the elevated legs dynamically as shown, combining lower-core engagement with hip-flexor motion.

  1. Keep the ribs controlled.
  2. Move the legs with control.
  3. Use a smaller range if the low back arches.
  4. Do not hold the breath.
6

Standing Torso Twist with Overhead Reach

Finish
Not specified in reel

Finish standing with a dynamic torso rotation and overhead reach. Keep the motion relaxed and coordinated.

  1. Stand tall before rotating.
  2. Let the arms follow the torso.
  3. Keep the feet grounded.
  4. Use a comfortable range.
