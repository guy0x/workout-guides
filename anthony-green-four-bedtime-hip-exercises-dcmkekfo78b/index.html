💡 How to follow: A silent four-part bedtime hip-mobility sequence shown on a bed: reclined butterfly, two supine hip-rotation variations, and a prone frog-like opener. The source supplies no reps, hold times, sides, or progression, so these are reference cards rather than a prescription.
1

Reclined Butterfly — Let the Hips Open

Drill 1
Reference demonstration; no source dosage

Lie on the back with the knees bent and dropping outward in the reclined butterfly position shown. Keep the range gentle and supported.

  1. Keep the pelvis comfortable
  2. Let gravity do less than you think
  3. Do not force the knees toward the bed
  4. Breathe slowly
2

Supine Hip Rotation — Wipers Without Forcing

Drill 2
Reference demonstration; no source dosage

From the supine setup, allow the legs to rotate inward and outward in the controlled windshield-wiper-style movement shown.

  1. Move from the hips
  2. Keep the low back comfortable
  3. Use a small range first
  4. Move slowly side to side
3

Supine Bent-Knee Rotation — Explore Internal Range

Drill 3
Reference demonstration; no source dosage

Lie on the back with the knees bent and explore the controlled hip-rotation pattern shown without forcing the low back.

  1. Keep the pelvis supported
  2. Avoid pinching at the front of the hip
  3. Use a small range
  4. Relax the legs enough to move
4

Prone Frog-Like Opener — Ease the Groin Open

Drill 4
Reference demonstration; no source dosage

From the prone position, open one bent knee to the side while the other leg stays longer, creating the supported hip/groin position shown.

  1. Keep the abdomen gently supported
  2. Do not lever the knee into the bed
  3. Stay below sharp groin pain
  4. Repeat both sides when appropriate
