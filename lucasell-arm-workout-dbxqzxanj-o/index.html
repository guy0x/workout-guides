1

Z-Bar Cable Curl

Biceps 1
3 sets × 8–12 reps

A strict cable curl using a Z-bar attachment. Keep the elbows controlled and use a full range rather than swinging the torso.

  1. Keep the elbows controlled
  2. Curl through a full range
  3. Avoid using momentum
2

Overhead Extension

Triceps 1
3 sets × 8–12 reps

An overhead cable extension emphasizing the long head of the triceps through a loaded stretch.

  1. Let the triceps lengthen under control
  2. Keep the upper arm position steady
  3. Extend without forcing the elbow
3

Rope Hammer Curl

Biceps 2
3 sets × 8–12 reps

A neutral-grip rope curl targeting the biceps and brachialis while maintaining constant cable tension.

  1. Use a neutral grip
  2. Keep the wrists aligned
  3. Control the return
4

Rope Tricep Extension

Triceps 2
3 sets × 8–12 reps

A rope pushdown-style triceps extension. Split the rope at the bottom and finish with a controlled lockout.

  1. Keep the elbows near the body
  2. Split the rope at the bottom
  3. Finish with a controlled lockout
5

Face-Away Cable Curl

Biceps 3
3 sets × 8–12 reps

A face-away curl with the cable behind the body, keeping tension through the arm and emphasizing peak contraction at the top.

  1. Face away from the cable
  2. Keep tension through the arm
  3. Squeeze at the top without leaning
6

Single-Arm Cable Extension

Triceps 3
3 sets × 8–12 reps

A unilateral cable extension to train each triceps side independently and limit side-to-side dominance.

  1. Work one arm at a time
  2. Keep the shoulder quiet
  3. Match control and range on both sides
