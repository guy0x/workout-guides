💡 How to follow: 4 movements, no equipment, ~20 min. Mastery in 20 Movements — part four.
1

Thoracic Rotational Arm Swing

Thoracic mobility
3 × 10 / side

Opens the upper spine and shoulder — undoes a day of sitting.

  1. Stand tall, rotate the torso
  2. Let the arm swing follow the rotation
  3. Breathe out on the rotation
2

Full-ROM Shoulder Arm Circles

Shoulder mobility
3 × 10 each direction

Big, controlled arm circles that keep the shoulder joint healthy and mobile.

  1. Arms out, draw big slow circles
  2. Lead with the fingertips, keep the ribs down
  3. Both directions, full range
3

McGregor Shoulder Swings

Shoulder · Rhythm
3 × 15

Loose, rhythmic shoulder swings that mobilize the joint and add a flow element.

  1. Relaxed shoulders, swing the arms
  2. Let the momentum come from the back
  3. Stay tall, breathe
4

Spinal Body Waves

Spine · Full-body flow
3 × 8 slow waves

A rolling wave through the whole spine — resets posture and mobility.

  1. Start tall, curl down vertebra by vertebra
  2. Roll back up slowly
  3. Breathe with the wave
