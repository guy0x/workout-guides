💡 How to follow: The full framework for a lean aesthetic physique: 4 gym days/week on an upper/lower split — each muscle hit twice weekly (higher frequency = more growth, no volume junk). Macros: protein grams = goal body-weight, fats 50–60 g, and the rest of a calorie deficit filled with carbs (don't cut them — they're the energy source). Meals are simple and repeatable: eggs + fruit + sourdough, grilled chicken + potatoes + avocado, ground beef + rice + veggies, Greek yogurt + whey + berries, and rice-cake + honey + banana pre-workout.
1

The Framework — Upper/Lower Split

Program

4 days a week, upper/lower split. The balance of getting lean without living in the gym. High intensity, minimal redundancy — the same muscles get hit multiple times a week, so each session stays short.

  1. 4 days/week — upper / lower / upper / lower
  2. High intensity, minimal redundancy
  3. Train for strength, not marathon sessions
2

Why Frequency Beats Volume

Science

Each muscle group is hit multiple times a week, so you don't need a ton of volume per session. Higher frequency = more muscle growth.

  1. Multiple weekly hits per muscle group
  2. Less volume per session needed
  3. Higher frequency → more growth
3

Macro Breakdown — Simple

Nutrition

Protein: your goal body weight, in grams, per day. Fats: 50–60 g/day. Everything leftover while staying in a calorie deficit = carbs. Carbs are key to performance and high energy — don't cut them.

  1. Protein = goal body weight (g/day)
  2. Fats 50–60 g/day
  3. Remaining calories → carbs
  4. Stay in a calorie deficit
4

The Meal Plan

Nutrition

Breakfast: eggs, egg whites, fruit, sourdough. Lunch: grilled chicken, any potatoes, avocado. Dinner: ground beef, rice, veggies. Snack: Greek yogurt, whey, berries. Pre-workout: rice cakes, honey, banana — fast and easily digestible.

  1. Breakfast — eggs, egg whites, fruit, sourdough
  2. Lunch — grilled chicken, potatoes, avocado
  3. Dinner — ground beef, rice, veggies
  4. Snack — Greek yogurt, whey, berries
  5. Pre-workout — rice cakes, honey, banana

Start Tomorrow

Habit

Simple, repeatable, effective meals and workouts — you can implement this into your life tomorrow. Three months of consistency changes your body.

  1. Repeatable meals, repeatable workouts
  2. Start implementing tomorrow
  3. 90 days of consistency = real change
