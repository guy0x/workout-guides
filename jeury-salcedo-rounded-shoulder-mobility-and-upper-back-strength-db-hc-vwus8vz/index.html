💡 How to follow: A narrated rounded-shoulder routine: chest/shoulder stretch, lat stretch, dumbbell face pull, and Y raise. The source prescribes 2–3 minutes daily for each stretch and 12 reps for 3 sets for the two strengthening exercises; the optional back bridge is not included as a card because setup and safety detail are incomplete.
1

Doorway or TRX Chest Stretch — Open the Front Line

Drill 1
2–3 minutes daily

Use a doorway at home or a TRX setup as shown. Lean into a controlled chest and shoulder stretch while intentionally improving the available range.

  1. Keep the neck relaxed
  2. Lean only into a pain-free range
  3. Do not force the shoulder forward
  4. Breathe steadily
2

Table Lat Stretch — Reach Through the Armpit

Drill 2
2–3 minutes daily

With the hands supported on a table, actively reach toward the wall and turn the elbows slightly inward to feel the lat area beneath the armpits.

  1. Reach actively rather than hanging passively
  2. Turn the elbows slightly inward
  3. Keep the ribs and low back controlled
  4. Use a stable table
3

Dumbbell Face Pull — Retract Without Shrugging

Drill 3
3×12

Use light dumbbells and bring the hands back with the elbows wide in a T shape. Engage the upper back and guide the shoulders back and down.

  1. Choose a light load
  2. Keep the elbows wide
  3. Avoid shrugging
  4. Move through controlled range
4

Y Raise — Reach Back with Scapular Control

Drill 4
3×12

Raise the arms behind the head in a Y shape while keeping the shoulders controlled. Reach back only as far as the shoulder range allows without pain or neck compensation.

  1. Keep the shoulders away from the ears
  2. Use minimal or no load initially
  3. Keep the neck neutral
  4. Stop before the low back arches
