💡 How to follow: A ten-move morning mobility flow moving from standing dynamic warm-up work into hip, spine, shoulder, and floor-based mobility. The creator frames it as 30 seconds per move; use that as the source's suggested rhythm, not as individualized medical or training advice.
1

Jumping Twists — Raise the Temperature

Move 1
30 seconds, as demonstrated

Use a light, rhythmic jumping twist to raise body temperature and introduce rotation. Keep the landing soft and reduce impact if needed.

  1. Land softly
  2. Rotate through the torso
  3. Keep the rhythm easy
  4. Scale the impact to your joints
2

Arm Circles — Restore Shoulder Range

Move 2
30 seconds, both directions

Circle the arms through a full comfortable range to prepare the shoulders. Reverse direction rather than forcing the end range.

  1. Use a pain-free arc
  2. Keep the ribs from flaring
  3. Circle both directions
  4. Move smoothly
3

Thoracic Chest Openers — Open the Front Line

Move 3
30 seconds, controlled repetitions

Sweep the arms to open the chest and rotate through the upper back. The transcript's “aura farmers” is an ASR error; the visual movement is a thoracic/chest opener.

  1. Move from the upper back
  2. Open the chest without arching the low back
  3. Keep the neck relaxed
  4. Stay controlled
4

McGregors — Clear the Upper-Body Cobwebs

Move 4
30 seconds, dynamic but controlled

Perform the creator's rotational McGregor-style warm-up swings to mobilize the shoulders and trunk. Keep the movement loose without throwing the joints.

  1. Stay light on the feet
  2. Let the trunk rotate
  3. Keep the shoulders relaxed
  4. Do not force the swing
5

Body Waves — Articulate the Spine

Move 5
30 seconds, slow flowing repetitions

Roll through the spine in a continuous body wave, moving segment by segment rather than collapsing into the low back.

  1. Move one spinal segment at a time
  2. Breathe continuously
  3. Keep the knees soft
  4. Avoid forcing the range
6

Yogi Lunges — Open the Hips and Challenge Balance

Move 6
30 seconds, alternate sides

Use the dynamic lunge pattern to open the hips while asking the legs, trunk, and balance system to coordinate.

  1. Step into a stable base
  2. Keep the front knee controlled
  3. Stay tall through the trunk
  4. Use support if balance is limited
7

Cossack Squats — Mobilize the Lower Body

Move 7
30 seconds, alternate sides

Shift into a lateral deep squat while extending the opposite leg. Use a shallower range or hold support if the hips, ankles, or balance are not ready.

  1. Keep the working foot grounded
  2. Track the knee with the toes
  3. Use a depth you control
  4. Hold support when needed
8

Pump Stretch / Human Cat Stretch — Flow Through Extension

Move 8
30 seconds, gentle flow

Flow between spinal extension and a rounded, folded position to move the spine through a comfortable range.

  1. Move slowly between positions
  2. Distribute the motion through the spine
  3. Keep the shoulders away from the ears
  4. Do not pinch the low back
9

Bridge / Wheel Pose — Open the Front Body

Move 9
30 seconds only if already prepared

The reel demonstrates a bridge/wheel backbend for the back, shoulders, and front body. This is an advanced position, not a default beginner mobility drill.

  1. Warm up before attempting
  2. Press evenly through hands and feet
  3. Keep the neck comfortable
  4. Use a regression or skip if unprepared
10

Windshield Wipers — Reset the Spine

Move 10
30 seconds, alternate sides

From a supine position, let the knees move side to side to finish with gentle spinal rotation and decompression.

  1. Keep the shoulders relaxed
  2. Move the knees together
  3. Use a comfortable rotation
  4. Breathe slowly
