Quadruped Frog

Hip Adductors
Not specified in reel

  1. Start on all fours, knees wide, feet together
  2. Sit hips back toward heels, forearms to ground
  3. Hold the deep stretch in the inner thighs
  4. Stay controlled — push knees out gently
2

Tall Kneeling Lean & Rotation

Hip Extension
Not specified in reel

  1. Kneel upright, knees hip-width, hands clasped at chest
  2. Lean torso back — feel the hip flexor stretch
  3. Rotate side to side from the hips, keep pelvis tall
  4. Do not arch the lower back
3

Seated 90-90

External / Internal Rotation
Not specified in reel

  1. Sit with one shin forward, the other back — both at 90°
  2. Torso stays tall, lean/rotate over the front leg
  3. Reach arm overhead and sweep across the body
  4. Switch sides and repeat
4

Half-Kneeling Lunge — Right

Hip Flexor Stretch
Not specified in reel

  1. Right foot forward, left knee down
  2. Torso upright, reach the arm overhead
  3. Drive hips forward gently for the stretch
  4. Keep the front knee over the ankle
5

Half-Kneeling Lunge — Left

Hip Flexor Stretch
Not specified in reel

  1. Left foot forward, right knee down
  2. Arms raised with elbows bent beside head
  3. Sink hips forward and stay tall
  4. Hold, then switch back
6

Deep Squat Hold

Ankle & Hip Mobility
Not specified in reel

  1. Feet wide, toes slightly out, drop into a full squat
  2. Palms pressed together, elbows inside the knees
  3. Push knees out, keep chest up and heels down
  4. Hold the bottom position and breathe
