💡 How to follow: A seated floor-mobility flow: straddle compression, a forward pancake fold, then a controlled cossack transition. These hinge positions build the hamstring length and core control behind every deep floor exercise.
1

Seated Straddle Compression Lift

Mobility

Seated straddle where you actively compress the legs toward the floor while keeping the torso tall — building the hamstring-brace and hip-hinge control needed for deep pancake work.

  1. Sit tall in a wide straddle
  2. Press the floor, draw legs tighter
  3. Keep spine long through the lift
2

Pancake Forward Fold Glide

Hinge

From the straddle, fold forward with a straight back and slide the hands along the floor — the classic pancake fold that opens hamstring range and trains a flat back hinge.

  1. Fold at the hips, back flat
  2. Walk hands forward, stay long
  3. Only fold as deep as form allows
3

Russian Cossack Transition Flow

Floor Flow

Side-to-side cossack transition — one leg folds under while the other stays long — linking the floor shapes into fluid, controlled movement.

  1. Shift weight side to side
  2. Keep the extended leg flat
  3. Move slow, stay in control
