1

Anywhere Reset

WARM-UP
1 round · ~5 min · no equipment

Full-body primer — warms the core, hips and spine before you load anything.

  1. Jumping Twists — 30s. Small hops, feet shoulder-width, swing the hips so knees twist opposite the shoulders. Stay tall, land soft.
  2. Cat–Cow (Pump Stretch) — 8 slow reps. Hands under shoulders, knees under hips. Exhale into a rounded cat, inhale into a tall cow. Move vertebrae by vertebrae.
  3. Cossack Squats — 6/side. Wide stance, toes slightly out. Sit into one side, other leg straight, push through the planted heel to stand.
  4. Standing Stomach Vacuum — 5 × 10s hold. Exhale all the air out while hollowing the belly in. Pull the navel toward the spine, breathe shallow while holding.
  5. Glute Bridge — 12 reps. Heels close to hips, drive through the heels, pause 1s at the top. Don't arch the lower back.

Band Goblet Squat

QUADS · CORE
4 × 10 reps

Replaces the kettlebell goblet squat — front-loaded resistance that teaches depth and core bracing.

  1. Stand on the middle of the band with feet shoulder-width, both handles looped up and held at your chest, elbows down.
  2. Sit between the hips, letting the elbows travel down inside the knees. Chest stays proud.
  3. Drive up through the heels, squeezing the glutes at the top.
  4. Slow the descent to 3 seconds — the eccentric is where the band gives you the most.
3

Band Bent-Over Row

BACK · PULL
4 × 10 reps

The main pulling movement of the session — treat it as the priority lift.

  1. Stand on the band with both feet, hinge forward until the torso is ~45°, back flat, core braced.
  2. Pull both handles to your hips/belly button, driving the elbows back tight against the ribcage.
  3. Squeeze the shoulder blades together for 1 second at the top, then lower under control.
  4. Alternate: anchor the band in a door hinge at chest height and row from a standing position for a different angle.
4

Band Swing

HIPS · POSTERIOR
4 × 12 reps

The hinge power move — the band's variable resistance actually suits a swing well.

  1. Stand on the band with both feet, handles held in front of the hips, arms relaxed.
  2. Hinge at the hips — not a squat. Push the hips back, soft knees, spine neutral. The band goes taut behind you.
  3. Snap the hips forward explosively. The arms are ropes — they just guide the handles up. Do not lift with the arms.
  4. Brace the abs hard and squeeze the glutes at the top. Let it return, hinge again.
5

Band Rotational High Pull

BACK · ROTATIONAL
3 × 10 reps / side

Rotation plus pull — explosive torso power and the oblique/back connection.

  1. Stand on the band with one foot, gripping the handle with the opposite hand.
  2. Drive the hips first, then pull high and rotate — lead with the elbow, not the hand.
  3. Finish with the hand around shoulder height, torso rotated through.
  4. Control the return slowly. Complete all reps on one side before switching.
6

Band Thruster

SHOULDERS · LEGS
3 × 8 reps

Full-body squat-to-press — the one move in this session that taxes everything at once.

  1. Stand on the band, handles racked at shoulder height, palms forward.
  2. Squat deep, chest proud, sitting back into the hips.
  3. Drive up explosively through the legs and press the handles overhead in one continuous motion — the legs launch the press.
  4. Lower the handles back to the rack position as you descend into the next squat. Keep the core braced the whole time.
7

Bosu Plank Rollout

CORE FINISHER
3 × 10 reps

The ab-wheel replacement. One of the highest-tension core movements available with no plates.

  1. Kneel in front of the bosu with your forearms on the dome, body in a straight line from knees to head.
  2. Roll the dome forward from the core, not by pushing with the shoulders. Keep the lower back flat — do not let it sag into hyperextension.
  3. Go only as far as you can hold the flat back. That's your range for today.
  4. Pull back with the abs, not the arms. 3-second return.
