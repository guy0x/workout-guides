💡 How to follow: Two floor-gymnastics positions: a pseudo-planche push-up and a tuck planche hold. Both build the shoulder transition, compression, and core tension upper-body calisthenics lives on.
1

Pseudo-Planche Push-Up

Strength

Push-up with the hips lifted high and hands pulled back toward the body — an advanced floor push-up that shifts load onto the shoulders and front of the torso.

  1. Hips high, hands under
  2. Shift weight forward over the hands
  3. Lower chest to floor, push up tall
2

Tuck Planche Isometric Hold

Hold

Tucked planche — knees tucked into the chest, the straight strong arms holding the body off the floor in the planche lean. The foundation of the full planche.

  1. Hands braced under the shoulders
  2. Tuck knees, chest up and tall
  3. Hold the lean, breathe slow
