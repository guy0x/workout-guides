Reverse Desk Posture — Extend What Sitting Folds

Move 1
30 sec. each! (source wording; meaning unspecified)

Use the beginner or advanced kneeling chest-and-spine extension shown. Move from the forward-desk posture into a controlled opening without forcing the low back.

  1. Keep the ribs controlled
  2. Extend through the upper back
  3. Choose the easier variation first
  4. Breathe without straining

Hammies + Hip Flexors — Lengthen Both Sides

Move 2
30 sec. each! (source wording; meaning unspecified)

Use a supported half-kneeling position to combine a hamstring stretch with hip-flexor work. Blocks are shown as optional support in the reel.

  1. Keep the front foot active
  2. Tuck the pelvis gently
  3. Use blocks to reduce strain
  4. Stay tall through the spine
3

Glutes + Outer Hip — Settle into a Supported Pigeon

Move 3
30 sec. each! (source wording; meaning unspecified)

Move into the supported pigeon-style position shown to address the glute and outer-hip area. Scale the front-leg angle to keep the knee comfortable.

  1. Support the torso with the hands
  2. Keep the front knee within a safe angle
  3. Do not force the hip toward the floor
  4. Switch sides evenly
4

Rounded-Shoulder Opener — Reclaim the Chest

Move 4
30 sec. each! (source wording; meaning unspecified)

Use the floor-based chest and shoulder-opening position shown in the beginner/advanced sequence. Keep the neck relaxed and avoid pinching at the front of the shoulder.

  1. Keep the shoulders away from the ears
  2. Use a pain-free range
  3. Do not arch aggressively through the low back
  4. Relax the jaw and neck
5

Lunge Thoracic Rotation — Mobilise the Spine

Move 5
30 sec. each! (source wording; meaning unspecified)

From the lunge-based setup, rotate through the upper back. The advanced version shown moves deeper into the twist; stay with the beginner range until control is reliable.

  1. Keep the lower body stable
  2. Rotate from the upper back
  3. Move slowly into the end range
  4. Use the beginner version if balance or hip range is limited
